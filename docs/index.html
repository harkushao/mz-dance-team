MZ Dance Team Praha

Dancing school

Martin and Zuzana have been dance partners since 1999. As multiple Czech Champions and World Vice-Champions in DanceSport, they bring years of experience and a genuine passion for dancing.

They founded their dance school to share what they love most, pass on their knowledge and help others grow as dancers. For them, dancing is not just a profession — it's a way of life.
