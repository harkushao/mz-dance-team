Meet Our Coaches

Get to know the people behind MZ Dance Team! Our coaches are here to guide you, share their knowledge and help you become a better dancer. They believe in hard work and focus, but that doesn't mean forgetting to have fun along the way. You'll get to know them even better at your first training session!

Ing. Martin Dvořák

Born on 16 April 1985, he has been dancing for almost as long as he can remember. Since 1999, he has shared the dance floor with Zuzana, his longtime dance partner.

What do you enjoy most about being a dance coach?
Seeing people improve, gain confidence and discover how much they can achieve. It's a great feeling to know that you can share something you love with others.

Is there a particular dance style you enjoy teaching the most?
I enjoy all of them for different reasons. Each dance has its own character, music and atmosphere, so it's hard to choose just one.

What advice would you give to someone stepping onto the dance floor for the first time?
Don't be afraid to make mistakes and, most importantly, enjoy yourself. Nobody starts as a professional. The important thing is to take that first step.

Zuzana Čejka Šilhánová

Born on 20 June 1985, she seems to have been dancing forever — perhaps even in a past life!

What does dance mean to you?
Dance is my passion. It brings me so much joy, although I have to admit that sometimes it can be quite a challenge too.

Which achievements mean the most to you?
Reaching second place at the Professional 10-Dance World Championships, making it to the semi-finals in Standard Dance at the World Championships and becoming Czech Champion several times are all milestones I'll always remember with pride.

What are your training sessions like?
I'm quite a perfectionist, so training with me isn't always easy! I believe that paying attention to every little detail is what makes the difference in the end.
